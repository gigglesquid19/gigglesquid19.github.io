Privacy Policy — Sharks vs Dolphins

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Sharks vs Dolphins game ("the app", package com.ciaranegan.sharksvsdolphins) published on Google Play by Ciaran Egan, an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("I", "me").

Short version

  • Your progress, Pearls, purchases and settings are saved on your device. I do not run a server that stores them, and I cannot see them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the EU), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later.
  • Purchases (Pearl Packs, Remove Ads, the paid Continue) are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • Leaderboards are on your device only. The app does not currently post scores online.
  • I do not sell your information, and the public version of the app sends no analytics to me.

Who is responsible for your information

For anything the app itself handles, the data controller is Ciaran Egan, contact Ciaegan94@gmail.com. Google is responsible for the information it collects through its own services (AdMob and Google Play), under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information stored on your device

To keep your game between sessions, the app saves the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your progress: levels and depths reached, checkpoints, achievements, tips seen, Sharkopedia entries and lifetime statistics (such as dolphins saved and play time);
  • your Pearl balance, Store upgrades and skins, consumables, the Home Reef's level, and your daily reward streak;
  • a record of purchases already credited (so a purchase is never credited twice), and whether ads have been removed;
  • your local high scores and the initials you enter for them;
  • the name you give your dolphin and, if you choose one, a country;
  • settings such as volume, mute and controls.

This stays on your device. It is not sent to me. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

No backup. The app does not allow its saved data to be included in Android backups or copied to a new phone, so it is never stored in your Google account backup. This also means your progress does not move with you to a new device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads (which you choose to watch, for example to double your Pearls or continue a run) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. They are provided by Google AdMob.

To serve, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID (the app declares the AD_ID permission for this);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads.

Consent. In regions where consent is required, including the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested, and asks for your choice about personalised ads. You can change your choice later: clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Sharks vs Dolphins > Storage) resets it, and the consent form is shown again the next time ads load. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on the device).

If you buy Remove Ads, the app stops requesting ads.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are made through Google Play. Google processes the payment and holds your payment details; I do not receive them. The app receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play (which product, and a purchase token), so it can credit what you bought. That confirmation is kept on your device as described above. Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice: https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice

Leaderboards

The game's leaderboards are kept on your device only, with the dolphin name or initials you enter. The app does not currently post scores to Google Play Games or any other online leaderboard. If online leaderboards are added later, this policy will be updated first.

Analytics

The public version of the app does not send analytics or usage data to me. If that changes, this policy will be updated first, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

Test versions

Before release, a small number of invited testers play test versions of the game. These are not the public app, but for completeness:

  • Tester data. Test builds ask testers whether they agree to share test data. Only if they say yes, the test build sends gameplay events (such as levels started and finished, times, and dolphins saved) and leaderboard results to a Google Sheet the developer uses for testing (via Google Apps Script). This includes the dolphin name and country the tester chose and a random ID created by the app on first use. It does not include the tester's real name, email address, or any device or advertising identifier. Testers can switch it off at any time from the pause menu. This data is used only to improve the game, is kept no longer than needed for testing, and is deleted on request.
  • Web test version. A browser version for testers is hosted on Cloudflare and protected by Cloudflare Access, which asks for the tester's email address to sign in. Cloudflare processes that address and standard access logs to control access, under Cloudflare's privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • Google Play testing. Testers who join a Google Play test track do so with their Google account; Google shares their test-track membership with the developer in the Play Console.

Links to other sites

The About screen links to Buy Me a Coffee (to support the game) and offers an email address for feedback. These open outside the app and are covered by those services' own privacy policies. If you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply and to improve the game, and I delete them on request.

Permissions

  • INTERNET: to load ads and make purchases. (Google's own libraries may add standard permissions they need, such as checking the network state.)
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: lets Google AdMob read the advertising ID, as described above.

The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Children

The app is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal information (for example by email), contact me and I will delete it.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EU, you have rights over your personal information, including to access it, correct it, delete it, and object to its use. Because the app keeps your game data on your device, you can delete it yourself by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For information held by Google (ads and Google Play), use Google's own tools at https://myaccount.google.com. For anything I hold (emails, or tester data), contact me at the address below. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Deleting your data

Sharks vs Dolphins, by Ciaran Egan, has no accounts. How to delete each kind of data:

  • Your game data (progress, Pearls, purchases credited, Home Reef, leaderboards, dolphin name, settings) is stored only on your device. Delete it yourself by clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Sharks vs Dolphins > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
  • Purchases stay on record with Google Play, because Google needs them for refunds and to restore Remove Ads. Payment records are kept by Google under its own rules, not by me.
  • Advertising data held by Google: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, and manage or delete Google's data at https://myaccount.google.com.
  • Anything I hold (emails you have sent me, or test data if you were a tester and agreed to share it): email Ciaegan94@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", and the dolphin name you used if you were a tester. I will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Nothing is kept after deletion.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to collect or share anything new, such as online saves or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
